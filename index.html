Participant table

Reads the participants tab of the Google Sheet the script lives in and publishes it to every host. Edit the sheet first, then publish.

Columns: WikiTreeID, Name, Team, Bib#, Image (optional bib image URL), Note. Anyone with something in Note is treated as ineligible (a previous marathon's winner or a WikiTree team member). Copying cells straight from a spreadsheet works too.
